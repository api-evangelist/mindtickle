# Mindtickle

> Mindtickle is an AI-powered revenue enablement platform that helps revenue teams sell smarter, coach more effectively, and engage modern buyers. Its agentic operating system, ElevateOS™, brings sales training, AI role play, coaching, conversation intelligence, sales content management, and Digital Sales Rooms together on one governed platform, so reps do on live deals what they already know they should do.

Mindtickle builds software for revenue enablement: the work of preparing, coaching, equipping, and guiding sales and go-to-market (GTM) teams, and of engaging their buyers. The platform covers the full enablement cycle. Teams use it to onboard and train reps, practice real conversations with AI role play, score and coach live calls, manage and share sales content, guide deals, and give buyers a branded Digital Sales Room. Mindtickle is used by more than 400 revenue teams and 1.2M users, including Cisco, Janssen, Splunk, Qlik, Juniper Networks, and World Wide Technology, and is ranked #1 by G2 for sales onboarding and training.

Mindtickle's central idea is the Know-Do Gap™: the structural gap between what sellers know and what they actually do in live deals. ElevateOS is built to close that gap. It replaces disconnected enablement tools with integrated, agentic execution, using AI agents that coach the gap, guide the deal, and act in the flow of work. ElevateOS rests on four pillars: Behavior Intelligence, Always-On Agentic Enablement, Intelligent Buyer Engagement, and an Interoperable Agentic Foundation. Mindtickle's tagline is "The art and science of sales."
