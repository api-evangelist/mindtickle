# Mindtickle

> Mindtickle is an AI-powered Revenue Enablement Platform used by B2B sales organizations to ramp reps faster, improve sales readiness, and drive consistent revenue performance. The platform combines sales training, coaching, content management, conversation intelligence, digital sales rooms, and AI roleplay into a unified system. Its flagship AI layer — ElevateOS — is an agentic operating system that learns from a decade of rep behavior data to coach, guide, and act on every deal.

Mindtickle serves VP/Director-level revenue leaders, sales enablement managers, sales operations, frontline sales managers, and L&D professionals at mid-market and enterprise B2B companies across industries including technology, automotive, medical devices, consumer goods, and chemicals.
